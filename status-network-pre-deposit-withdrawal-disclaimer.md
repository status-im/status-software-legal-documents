## 2. Background

As part of the anticipated mainnet launch of Status Network, people were able to deposit assets, including SNT (Ethereum L1), Linea (Linea L2), ETH and certain US stablecoins (Ethereum L1) into non-custodial smart contract vaults, being SNT vault, Linea vault, WETH vault and GUSD vault, as part of a pre-deposit phase. Certain assets, namely in the WETH and GUSD vaults accrued yield and in addition, pre-depositors were also potentially entitled to certain liquid rewards in the form of SNT and/or Linea Tokens.  

Assets deposited by pre-depositors were locked in the vaults pending bridging to Status Network upon its mainnet launch. However, with the now intended [integration](https://status.network/blog/status-network-merges-with-linea-scaling-gasless-privacy-upstream) of Status Network codebase into the Linea infrastructure, the vaults and corresponding assets will now be bridged to Linea Mainnet. 

Upon bridging to Linea Mainnet and the vaults being open, pre-depositors will be able to withdraw their assets, along with being distributed, as applicable, accrued yield and liquid rewards as earlier [indicated](https://status.network/blog/pre-deposit-vaults-withdrawal-timeline-and-rewards-distribution).
